# Canonical Scientific Textbooks and Standards

## Overview
This document catalogs the authoritative textbooks, international standards organizations, and peer-reviewed reference compendiums that govern ATOMICA's scientific validation. Per Section 18.7 and Section 19.5, no citation may be fabricated or drawn from unverified secondary sources.

---

## 1. Official Standards Organizations & Compendiums

1. **IUPAC (International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry)**
   - *Compendium of Chemical Terminology (The Gold Book)*: Authoritative definitions for bond order, aromaticity, electronegativity, and reaction kinetics.
   - *Standard Atomic Weights (2021)*: Precision atomic weights for all 118 elements.
   - URL: [https://iupac.org/](https://iupac.org/)
2. **NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology)**
   - *NIST Chemistry WebBook (SRD 69)*: Thermochemical data ($\Delta H_f^\circ, S^\circ, C_p$), vibrational frequencies, ionization energies.
   - *NIST Atomic Spectra Database*: Energy levels and ground state electron configurations.
   - URL: [https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/](https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/)
3. **CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics (104th ed., 2023–2024)**
   - Comprehensive experimental tables: bond dissociation energies, dipole moments, aqueous solubilities, vapor pressures.
4. **Worldwide Protein Data Bank (wwPDB / RCSB PDB)**
   - High-resolution X-ray crystallography, Cryo-EM, and NMR macromolecular coordinate standards.
   - URL: [https://www.rcsb.org/](https://www.rcsb.org/)

## 2. Foundational Textbooks by Discipline

### Physical & Quantum Chemistry
- Atkins, P., de Paula, J., & Keeler, J. (2018). *Atkins' Physical Chemistry* (11th ed.). Oxford University Press.
- Levine, I. N. (2014). *Quantum Chemistry* (7th ed.). Pearson.

### Inorganic & Structural Chemistry
- Gillespie, R. J., & Hargittai, I. (1991). *The VSEPR Model of Molecular Geometry*. Prentice Hall.
- Miessler, G. L., Fischer, P. J., & Tarr, D. A. (2014). *Inorganic Chemistry* (5th ed.). Pearson.

### Organic Chemistry & Cheminformatics
- Clayden, J., Greeves, N., & Warren, S. (2012). *Organic Chemistry* (2nd ed.). Oxford University Press.
- Leach, A. R. (2001). *Molecular Modelling: Principles and Applications* (2nd ed.). Pearson.

### Biochemistry & Molecular Biology
- Nelson, D. L., & Cox, M. M. (2021). *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry* (8th ed.). W. H. Freeman.
- Alberts, B., et al. (2022). *Molecular Biology of the Cell* (7th ed.). W. W. Norton & Co.

### Physiology & Immunology
- Hall, J. E., & Hall, M. E. (2020). *Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology* (14th ed.). Elsevier.
- Murphy, K., & Weaver, C. (2016). *Janeway's Immunobiology* (9th ed.). Garland Science.
