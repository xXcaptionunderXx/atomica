Molecules

    How to use
    • Drag an element from the table into the sandbox.
    • Drop it on an atom to bond it there.
    • Click an atom, then another atom, to bond them.
    • With an atom selected, click an element in the table to attach it.
    • Easy: double/triple bonds are chosen for you. Hard: click a bond (or bond the same pair again) to cycle single → double → triple; red bonds have the wrong order.
    • Geometry grid: molecules snap to their real VSEPR shape with bond angles, wedges (toward you), dashes (away) and lone-pair dots. Drag moves the whole molecule.
    • Right-click or Delete removes.

    Bonding uses common valences as an approximation; it ignores formal charges, coordinate bonds and ionic/metallic character. Dashed ring = unfilled valence. Angles are idealised VSEPR values (real molecules differ slightly, e.g. H₂S is 92°). Names are matched by formula, so isomers are listed as alternatives.
