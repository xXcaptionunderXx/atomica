Molecules

    How to use
    • Drag an element from the table into the sandbox.
    • Molecule library (left): search 1000+ real biomolecules, vitamins and mineral forms by name, synonym or formula; click one to add it, or drag it to a spot. Structures come from PubChem.
    • Drop it on an atom to bond it there.
    • Click an atom, then another atom, to bond them.
    • With an atom selected, click an element in the table to attach it.
    • Easy: double/triple bonds are chosen for you. Hard: click a bond (or bond the same pair again) to cycle single → double → triple; red bonds have the wrong order.
    • Geometry grid: molecules snap to their real VSEPR shape with bond angles, wedges (toward you), dashes (away) and lone-pair dots. Drag moves the whole molecule.
    • Right-click or Delete removes.
    • Move around: drag empty space to pan, scroll to zoom (sandbox only); Fit or F frames everything.
    • Select several: Shift+drag a box, or Ctrl+click atoms. Drag any selected atom to move them all; Delete or right-click removes them all.
    • Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut, paste (at the mouse) · Ctrl+A select all · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.

    Bonding uses common valences as an approximation; it ignores formal charges, coordinate bonds and ionic/metallic character. Dashed ring = unfilled valence. Angles are idealised VSEPR values (real molecules differ slightly, e.g. H₂S is 92°). Names are matched by formula, so isomers are listed as alternatives. Library molecules keep PubChem's formal charges (shown as ⁺/⁻ badges); ionic compounds such as calcium carbonate appear as their separate ions.
