## 1. Primary Physical & Quantum Chemistry

1. **Atkins, P., de Paula, J., & Keeler, J.** (2018). *Atkins' Physical Chemistry* (11th ed.). Oxford University Press.
   - Core reference for chemical thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, Arrhenius equations, and quantum mechanics of atoms.
2. **Gillespie, R. J., & Hargittai, I.** (1991). *The VSEPR Model of Molecular Geometry*. Prentice Hall.
   - Foundational text for steric numbers, lone-pair repulsive orders, and molecular geometry prediction.
3. **Pauling, L.** (1960). *The Nature of the Chemical Bond and the Structure of Molecules and Crystals* (3rd ed.). Cornell University Press.
   - Reference for electronegativity scales, covalent and ionic radii, and resonance theory.
4. **NIST Chemistry WebBook.** NIST Standard Reference Database Number 69. National Institute of Standards and Technology. [https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/](https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/)
   - Authoritative source for thermochemical constants, gas-phase ion energetics, and molecular masses.

## 2. Cheminformatics & Computational Modeling

1. **Leach, A. R.** (2001). *Molecular Modelling: Principles and Applications* (2nd ed.). Pearson Education.
   - Reference for molecular mechanics, empirical force fields (MMFF, UFF), energy minimization, and distance geometry.
2. **Weininger, D.** (1988). *SMILES, a chemical language and information system. 1. Introduction to methodology and encoding rules*. *Journal of Chemical Information and Computer Sciences*, 28(1), 31-36.
   - The canonical definition of SMILES notation.
3. **Landrum, G. et al.** (2024). *RDKit: Open-source cheminformatics software*. [https://www.rdkit.org/](https://www.rdkit.org/)
   - Algorithms for canonicalization, substructure matching, and 2D coordinate generation.
4. **Kim, S. et al.** (2023). *PubChem 2023 update*. *Nucleic Acids Research*, 51(D1), D1373–D1380.
   - Specification for PUG-REST interfaces and compound property schemas.

## 3. Biophysics, Protein Structure & Cellular Biology

1. **Nelson, D. L., & Cox, M. M.** (2021). *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry* (8th ed.). W. H. Freeman.
   - Reference for amino acid structures, peptide bonds, metabolic pathways (Glycolysis, Krebs cycle), and enzyme kinetics.
2. **Ramachandran, G. N., Ramakrishnan, C., & Sasisekharan, V.** (1963). *Stereochemistry of polypeptide chain configurations*. *Journal of Molecular Biology*, 7(1), 95-99.
   - The foundational definition of permissible backbone dihedral angles ($\phi, \psi$).
3. **Alberts, B. et al.** (2022). *Molecular Biology of the Cell* (7th ed.). W. W. Norton & Company.
   - Core reference for membrane transport, organelle function, ATP synthesis, and cell cycle regulation.
4. **Berman, H. M. et al.** (2000). *The Protein Data Bank*. *Nucleic Acids Research*, 28(1), 235-242.
   - Structural coordinate standard for macromolecular visualization.

## 4. Physiology, Systems Biology & Immunology

1. **Hall, J. E., & Hall, M. E.** (2020). *Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology* (14th ed.). Elsevier.
   - Reference for Windkessel hemodynamics, alveolar gas exchange, and renal filtration equations.
2. **Murphy, K., & Weaver, C.** (2016). *Janeway's Immunobiology* (9th ed.). Garland Science.
   - Canonical reference for innate vs. adaptive immune cell cascades, antigen presentation, and antibody affinity maturation.
3. **Hucka, M. et al.** (2003). *The systems biology markup language (SBML): a medium for representation and exchange of biochemical network models*. *Bioinformatics*, 19(4), 524-531.
   - Standard data format for metabolic and gene regulatory network simulations.
